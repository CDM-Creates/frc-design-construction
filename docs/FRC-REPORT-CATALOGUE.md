Each entry declares template ID, intended customers, answered question, inclusions, exclusions, input/reference/drawing rules, outputs, limitations, combination rule, full price, shared credit and minimum contribution. Development reports include the property/planning baseline, area and boundary status, motivation, development analysis, concept visuals, constraints, services, sources, missing information, risks and actions. They exclude measured or construction drawings, engineering/survey certification, approval guarantees and copied third-party designs.

## Minimum launch deliverables

| Price band | Required value |
| --- | --- |
| A$695–A$895 | Approximately 15–25 professionally formatted pages; property identity; planning framework; zoning and available controls; mapped-constraint screening; opportunity summary; source register; missing-information schedule; risk register; action plan. |
| A$995–A$1,495 | Everything in the property report plus a detailed development-specific assessment, planning-control matrix, approval-pathway analysis, development risks, consultant pathway, recommendations and evidence/limitation labels. |
| A$1,495–A$1,795 | Several options or a complex proposal; meaningful comparison; site-demand conflicts; combined constraints; recommendation hierarchy; risk comparison; more extensive control analysis; uploaded-document integration when purchased and supplied. |
| From A$2,195 professionally reviewed | Human review; corrections; professional commentary; review record; audit trail; release approval; and a clear distinction from the AI-assisted draft. |

A premium report must not be padded with repeated disclaimers, generic planning explanations, unsupported conclusions or copied council wording. The output must organise property-specific evidence and state what the client should investigate next.

The first fixed-price report is full price. Eligible additional reports receive their configured shared-property research credit without reducing review minimums. Authoritative land-area tiers are: up to 1,000 m² included; 1,001–2,000 A$195; 2,001–5,000 A$395; 5,001–10,000 A$695; above 10,000, multiple adjoining parcels, rural/agricultural or unusual land requires quotation. Approximate, unavailable or conflicting area never creates an automatic surcharge.

The server applies the professional/council minimum before priority and chargeable document-analysis upgrades, so those selected upgrades always remain visible and additive. Analysis already included by Plan Compliance, Council Readiness or Complex Development is never charged twice. The server recalculates immediately before checkout and stores an immutable hash-addressed snapshot. Browser and AI totals are ignored.
