# Australian report reference research

Access date for all references: 30 July 2026.

The references below informed information architecture only. FRC does not copy third-party branding, diagrams or substantial wording.

## 1. Apply for a development application

- Publisher: Service NSW
- URL: <https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/apply-for-a-development-application>
- Document type: Official NSW development-application process and document overview
- Useful structures: clear prerequisites, document list, online lodgement sequence and payment boundary
- Ideas adopted: separate document-readiness and payment stages; do not describe an application as lodged before the relevant payment/event
- Ideas not adopted: a government transaction layout or wording
- Copyright and reuse: link and paraphrase only

## 2. Check document requirements for your planning application

- Publisher: City of Sydney
- URL: <https://www.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/development-applications/check-document-requirements-development-application>
- Document type: NSW council submission requirements and report glossary
- Useful structures: proposal-dependent document requirements, drawing metadata, SEE contents and specialist-report triggers
- Ideas adopted: document register, revision/date/author metadata, planning-control matrix and explicit missing-document schedule
- Ideas not adopted: treating one council checklist as universally applicable across NSW
- Copyright and reuse: link and paraphrase only; council requirements must be rechecked at report time

## 3. Steps to prepare a DA

- Publisher: Ku-ring-gai Council
- URL: <https://www.krg.nsw.gov.au/Planning-and-development/Development-applications-DA/Steps-to-prepare-a-DA>
- Document type: NSW council DA preparation pathway
- Useful structures: compliance check, plans/documents, specialist inputs, lodgement and assessment as distinct phases
- Ideas adopted: staged action plan and consultant pathway
- Ideas not adopted: fixed processing-time representations or council-specific requirements outside that LGA
- Copyright and reuse: link and paraphrase only

## 4. State significant development guidelines — preparing a scoping report

- Publisher: NSW Department of Planning and Environment
- URL: <https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-03/ssd-guidelines-preparing-a-scoping-report.pdf>
- Document type: Official NSW scoping-report guidance
- Useful structures: project description, site context, statutory framework, matters requiring assessment, engagement and evidence gaps
- Ideas adopted: source register, constraints/opportunities split, risk-focused scoping and proportionate evidence
- Ideas not adopted: state-significant-development scale, statutory status or department branding
- Copyright and reuse: NSW material permits linking; this commercial product uses an original structure and wording

## 5. Development referrals guide

- Publisher: NSW Department of Planning and Environment
- URL: <https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-03/development-referrals-guide.pdf>
- Document type: Official NSW concurrence, referral and integrated-development guide
- Useful structures: issue identification, responsible authority, trigger, evidence and next action
- Ideas adopted: consultant/authority pathway and responsible-party columns in the action plan
- Ideas not adopted: automatically asserting that a referral applies before professional confirmation
- Copyright and reuse: link and paraphrase only

## 6. Development application document checklist — signage

- Publisher: Inner West Council
- URL: <https://www.innerwest.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-02/DA%20Checklist%20-%20Signage.pdf>
- Document type: Current NSW council checklist example
- Useful structures: “required” versus “may be required” evidence status
- Ideas adopted: deterministic coverage statuses and visible missing-information states
- Ideas not adopted: signage-specific scope and checklist wording
- Copyright and reuse: link and paraphrase only
