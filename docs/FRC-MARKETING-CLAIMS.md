# FRC marketing claims

The authoritative copy is in `marketing-content.ts`.

Approved themes are detailed planning intelligence before a fully bespoke investigation; transparent pre-payment pricing; no charge merely for uploads; comparison of inclusions/evidence/deliverables; reduced repeated research and document sorting; and use of evidence the client already holds.

Eligible AI-only delivery is a target: “Target generation time: approximately 10–20 minutes after payment, successful file processing and access to the required property sources.” It must be qualified for unavailable sources, file processing, conflicts and professional review. Professional-review timing begins after the draft and required information are available.

Do not claim cheapest in Australia, guaranteed cheaper, that every firm charges more, every possible fact is included, guaranteed approval, guaranteed delivery, investment return, certified design or construction-ready visuals.

Comparative price wording stays hidden unless an administrator supplies approved wording, source, comparison date, comparable-service definition and future expiry date. Missing or expired evidence returns no claim.

Owners receive plain-English buildability and consultant sequencing. Buyers receive acquisition and constraint questions. Investors receive options, risks and investigations without return promises. Builders receive dependencies and missing inputs. External designers receive control matrices, plan conflicts and coordination. Agents receive a source-labelled referral path without guarantees.

Every report includes one factual-correction request for wrong property, transcription, omitted upload, incorrect attribution or obvious generation error. Clarification, professional upgrade, detailed review and complaint are separate. Disputes do not guarantee refunds or changed conclusions; corrections require outcome, revision and change records.

Before launch FRC should obtain legal review of terms, privacy, refunds/disputes, service limitations, professional titles/registrations, comparisons, targets and Australian Consumer Law risk.
